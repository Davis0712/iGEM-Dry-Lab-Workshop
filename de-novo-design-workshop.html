Proteins play a central role in biological processes. Nearly everything your body does — moving, digesting, healing — is run by proteins.
A receptor (受體) is a protein receiver. It sits on a cell and recognises one specific signal — like a lock waiting for its key.
Your immune system (免疫系統) is the body's defence. When something is wrong, it triggers an alarm — and other cells answer the call.
The Problem

A wound that won't stop alarming

A chronic wound keeps triggering an immune alarm. The alarm travels through a receptor called MD2 — a protein built from amino acids.
TAP2 already blocks MD2 — and helps wounds heal.
But TAP2 is flawed:
short-livedonly moderately strongcan provoke an immune response
Our job: design a better TAP2 — with generative AI, not from nothing.
Then run it through the same filters a real drug candidate faces.

Stage 2 · Filter · Step 3 of 10

Throw away anything that won't fold

confident — the shape is probably realuncertain — the model isn't sure
pLDDT · keep ≥ 70, discard below
AlphaFold gives every design a confidence: pLDDT, scored 0–100. It is not a grade for the protein — it is the model's own estimate of how close its prediction will land.
A confident region means the shape is probably real. An uncertain region means the model is guessing — that backbone may not exist at all.
survived · pLDDT ≥ 70
8 of 10
Confidence is not quality — but a design nobody can fold has no chance at all. That is why it is the cheapest check, run first.

Stage 3 · Physics · Step 7 of 10

Does it stay stable at body temperature?

Two candidates remain. Drop both into a simulated water box at 37 °C and follow their motion for 20 ns. RMSD measures how far each drifts from its starting shape.
MD2B-01 — persistent rise: substantial drift. A warning sign.
MD2B-10 — low plateau: limited drift. We take this one forward.

Stage 3 · Physics · Step 8 of 10

Make the grip stronger

MD2B-10 holds on, but weakly: ΔG = −6.6 kcal/mol. ΔG is the binding free energy (結合自由能) — the more negative, the tighter the grip. We need ≤ −8.0. Mutate one residue at a time — never the five locked positions that touch the hotspots.
